Our Story

Everyone gets a seat at this table

Vikings started as Sunday dinners for whoever showed up — cousins, neighbors, the whole street. We just built a restaurant around it. Same big platters, same long tables, same standing rule: nobody leaves hungry.

Kids get their own board. Grandparents get the good seat by the hearth. That's the whole menu philosophy, really.
